Notes are written where they belong: on a task, a scene, a shot, a shoot day or a call. **Project notes** gathers them all in one list so nothing gets lost.

{{shots notes}}

## Project notes

Open **Project notes** in the project's menu. It lists every note on the project, newest first:

- Task notes and scene notes.
- Character notes.
- Shot notes.
- A shoot day's title note and call sheet notes.
- Notes in a day's schedule and on people's calls.
- Team member notes, for admins and the person they're about.

Each line says who wrote it and when, and where it was taken. Select **Where** to open that item with its form open, and edit the note there.

Filters and search cover every note, and the list pages under the table.

## Who can change a note

- Notes on tasks, scenes and characters: anyone on the project adds them; the author edits their own; the author or an admin deletes one.
- Notes kept in a field (a shot, a shoot day, a call) are edited in that item's form, and record who last changed them.

## Journal

The project journal is coming soon. It will hold dated entries that are either **private** (only you, unless you share them with chosen people) or **public** (everyone on the project).
