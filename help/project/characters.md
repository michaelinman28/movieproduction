**Characters** lists everyone in the story. Cast numbers set the order of the cast on call sheets, and each character's actor links the story to the team.

{{shots characters}}

## Add a character

1. Open **Characters** in the project's menu.
2. Type the name and tap **Add character**.
3. Fill in the form and tap **Save character**.

## The character's form

- **Basics**: name, cast number (1 for the lead), type (lead, supporting, day player or background), age range, gender, description, traits and arc.
- **Casting**: status (open, auditioning, offered or cast), and the actor, stand-in and stunt double, picked from the team. Picking an actor marks the character cast.
- **Scenes**: the scenes they're in, each with a note on how they look in that scene.
- **Look and continuity**: what must match from shot to shot, and the elements that belong to them, such as wardrobe and props.
- **Notes** and, for admins, **History**.

## Who plays whom

Who plays a character is stored once. The Characters page (Actor), the Team page (Characters) and a shoot day's calls (Characters) all show and change that same link. Picking a character someone else plays moves it to the new person.

## On the call sheet

Each person's line shows the characters they play, such as "Ellie (#1)". The cast list and each scene's cast run in cast-number order.

## Delete a character

Whoever added it, or an admin, taps **Delete character** in its form. Its elements stay.

## Rules

- Names are unique in the project and can't contain commas.
- Cast numbers are unique in the project.
- The actor, stand-in and double must be on the team.
