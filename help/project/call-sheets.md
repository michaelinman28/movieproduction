The call sheet is built from the shoot day and everything it points to: its locations, scenes, characters, elements and calls. You check it, send a version, and watch confirmations come in.

{{shots call-sheet}}

## Open it

On **Shoot days**, select a day and tap **Open call sheet**. The Overview's next shoot day links to it too.

## What's on it

- The project, day number, date, title and title note, with the crew call.
- Times: crew call, shooting call, lunch and estimated wrap.
- Each location with its address (opens maps), parking and on-site contact, and the nearest hospital.
- Weather, sunrise and sunset, and key contacts found by role.
- The schedule: scenes with their location, cast and elements, plus breaks, and the page total.
- The cast list in cast-number order, and the crew by department.
- Dietary needs for catering, allergies first.
- Notes, and the next day's advance schedule.

The times, locations, weather, hospital and key contacts sit together under **Day details**. Like every other part of the sheet, tap its heading to fold it; its heading shows the shooting call and how many locations. Each device remembers what's folded.

## Send it

1. Check the sheet.
2. Under **Send**, tap **Send version 1**.
3. Tell people: **Email everyone** opens your email app with everyone called in Bcc, or **Copy message** copies it to paste anywhere. Or send each person their own link: see [Personal and shared links](/help/project/call-sheet-links).

If you change the day afterwards, send the next version. Everyone is asked to confirm again.

## Confirmations

Each person's call shows:

- **Waiting**: hasn't opened it.
- **Viewed**: opened it.
- **Confirmed**: tapped **Confirm I got it**.
- **Confirmed an old version**: confirmed before you sent a new one.

People called confirm their own call at the top of the sheet.

## Cast and crew views

- **Crew** see the full sheet.
- **Cast** see **My call**: their call, the schedule with their scenes highlighted, the location, weather and hospital.
- People who are both get a **My call / Full sheet** switch.

Who counts as cast is explained in [Roles](/help/company/roles).

## Print or save as PDF

Tap **Print or save as PDF**. The sheet is laid out on US Letter paper in light colors, always with every part open, even ones folded on screen.

### Choose the printed layout

Each project picks how its call sheets print, under **Call sheet print** in **Edit project** on the Overview (or in the Projects table on the company page):

- **Modern**: the full design you see on screen, usually two pages.
- **One page**: the whole day on a single page: times, locations, hospital and key contacts across the top, then the schedule, cast and crew as compact tables, and elements, notes and the next day at the bottom. A very long day is shrunk to fit.
- **Both**: you get two buttons, **Print modern layout** and **Print one page**, and each prints only its own layout.

The same choice applies to personal and shared links.
