The **Team** is everyone working on this project. Only people on the team can be given tasks, play characters, own elements or be called for a shoot day.

{{shots team}}

## Add people to the team

Company admins:

1. Open **Team** in the project's menu.
2. Choose someone from **Choose someone from the company…** and tap **Assign to project**.
3. For someone who isn't in the company yet, open **Add someone who isn't in the company yet**, give their email and name, and tap **Add to project**. They're added to the company too.

Their company roles are copied to the project as their **Project roles**.

## A person's form

Select someone to open their form under the table:

- **Project roles**: what they do on this project. Admins change them.
- **Characters**: the characters they play. This is the same link as **Actor** on the Characters page and **Characters** on a shoot day's calls, so changing it in one place changes it everywhere.
- **Dietary needs**: needs for catering, with a box to mark an allergy. Set by the person or an admin. Shown on this project's call sheets.
- **Team notes**: dated notes about the person, such as availability or a contract note. Only company admins and the person can see them.
- **History**, for admins.

Tap **Save** to keep changes. Name, phone and email belong to the person's profile, so they're changed on the company's People list.

## Team note types

Admins keep the list of note types (General, Availability and so on) in the **Team note types** section. Add a type with **Add note type**, or rename or delete one.

## Take someone off the project

In their form, an admin taps **Remove from project**. Their tasks become unassigned, and they come off any characters, elements and calls on this project.
