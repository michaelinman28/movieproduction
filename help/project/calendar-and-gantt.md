Choose **Calendar** in the Tasks page's **Show** menu, then switch between **Month** and **Gantt chart**. Your choice is remembered on your device.

{{shots calendar}}

## Month

- Each day lists the tasks due that day, in order of the day. On a phone, each day shows a count instead.
- Tap a day to list its tasks under the month, and tap a task to open its form.
- Tasks you add while a day is picked are due that day.
- On a computer, drag a task to another day to move its due date. It saves at once.
- Tasks with no due date are counted under the month.

## Gantt chart

- Days and months run across the top; tasks run down the left in sub-task order, with sub-tasks indented and foldable.
- A task with a start and due date is a bar from start to finish. A task on one day is a diamond.
- Done tasks are grey, overdue ones red, and a line marks today. The chart opens at today and scrolls sideways.
- Tap a task to open its form, or tap a day to add a task on it.
- On a computer, drag a bar to move the task by whole days. It saves at once.

## Tips

- Give prep tasks a start date as well as a due date so they show as bars.
- Use the Gantt chart to check that permits and fittings finish before the shoot days they're for.
