Links let anyone read a sent call sheet without signing in. There are two kinds. Both appear on the call sheet page once a version is sent.

{{shots links}}

## Personal links

Each person called has their own private link. It shows the sheet with their call at the top, and lets them tap **Confirm I got it**.

Under **Personal links**, for each person:

- **Open** shows their link.
- **Copy link** copies it.
- **Email** opens your email app with a message to them.
- **Text** opens your messages app.

A personal link stops working when the person is taken off the day. Someone who is only cast sees just their own call on it.

## Shared link

One link to the whole day's sheet, for anyone: a location owner, a vendor, a day player. It's view only, with no confirming.

1. Under **Shared link**, tap **Turn on shared link**.
2. Tick **Show phone numbers** to include cast, crew and on-site contact phones. They're hidden otherwise.
3. **Open**, **Copy link**, **Email** or **Text** it.

**Make a new link** stops the old one working. **Turn off** stops it altogether. The shared link always shows the latest sent version.

## What links never show

No email addresses, ever. The shared link never shows dietary needs. Opening either link is recorded in the Activity log.

## What people see

The page people open is explained, for them, in [Your call sheet link](/help/getting-started/your-call-sheet-link). It has a "What is this?" link to that page.
