Each shoot day is one date on the schedule. You set its times and locations, put scenes in order and call people. Its call sheet is built from all of it.

{{shots shoot-days}}

## Add a shoot day

1. Open **Shoot days** in the project's menu.
2. Pick the date and tap **Add shoot day**.
3. The day's form opens under the table, with three parts: Details, Schedule and Who's called.

Days are numbered by date: "Day 1 of 3".

## Details

Changes here wait for **Save day**.

- **Date**, **title** and a **title note** shown under the title on the call sheet.
- **Locations**, in order. Add from the list, move one up, or take it off the day. The first is the main location: where the crew call is, and whose weather is looked up.
- **Crew call**, **shooting call**, **lunch** and **estimated wrap**.
- **Weather**, **sunrise** and **sunset**. Tap **Look up weather, sunrise and sunset** to fill them in for the main location. It works for dates up to about 16 days ahead.
- **Notes** for the call sheet.

## Schedule

The day's run, in order. Add scenes from the Scenes page, meal breaks, company moves and notes, each with an optional time. Drag to reorder.

- A scene can be on one day. Putting it on a day marks it **Scheduled**.
- If a scene's location isn't on the day yet, it's added at the end of the day's locations.

## Who's called

Add people from the team one by one, or tap **Call everyone**. For each person:

- **Call** time. Blank means the crew call.
- **Characters** they play, which makes them cast.
- **Pickup** and **makeup** times, and a **note**.

Each line also shows whether they've viewed and confirmed the sent sheet: Waiting, Viewed, Confirmed, or Confirmed an old version.

## Next

Tap **Open call sheet** to check and send it. See [Call sheets](/help/project/call-sheets).

## Who can change it

Crew on the project plan shoot days. Cast can read them but not change them. Whoever added a day, or an admin, can delete it.
