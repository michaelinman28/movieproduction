Each scene has its own shot list, in the order you'll shoot. On set, tick each shot **Done** as it's in the can.

{{shots shot-list}}

## Where to find it

On **Scenes**, select a scene. Its form opens under the table, with **Shot list** below **Details**.

## Add shots

1. Under **Shot list**, describe the shot and tap **Add shot**.
2. Fill in its size, angle, movement, lens, equipment, estimated minutes and notes, in the table or the shot's form.
3. Drag the handle at the left to change the order.

Size, angle and movement are pick-lists: for example Wide, Close-up or POV; Eye level or Low; Static, Dolly or Drone.

## On set

Tap the **Done** box on a shot's row. It saves at once. The Scenes table shows each scene's shots done, and the project's Overview counts shots done across the project.

## Tips

- The estimated minutes add up to each scene's shot time, which helps when building a shoot day.
- Shot notes show on [Project notes](/help/project/project-notes-and-journal) with a link back to the scene.
