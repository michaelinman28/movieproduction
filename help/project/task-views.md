The **Show** menu at the top of Tasks changes how the same tasks are laid out. Your choice is remembered on your device.

{{shots task-views}}

## The views

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Sub-task tree | Every task with its sub-tasks folded under it. Tap the arrow to open or close a branch |
| Calendar | A month by due date, or a Gantt chart. See [Calendar and Gantt chart](/help/project/calendar-and-gantt) |
| Overdue | Only late tasks that aren't done, oldest first. The menu shows how many |
| Grouped by person, category, location, status, priority or due date | Flat lists in groups, with a count per group |
| My tasks | Only tasks assigned to you, soonest due first. Tasks you add here are assigned to you |

In grouped views, drag another column header into the grouping bar to group by that too.

## Search and filters

- **Quick filter**, above the table, finds tasks by any word in them. **Expand** and **Collapse** open or close every branch of the tree.
- Each column has a filter under its header. Columns with fixed values, such as status and person, filter with a list of tick boxes; tags filter by each tag.
- Tap a column header to sort.

## Columns

Tap **Columns** above the table to choose which columns show. See [Tables](/help/working/tables).

Switching views closes the open task form, so save first.
