Everyone who works with your company is on its **People** list, whether or not they have signed up yet. You add them once, and then put them on any of your projects.

{{shots people}}

## Add someone

1. On the company page, open **People**.
2. Type their **Email**.
3. If they haven't signed up yet, also type their **Name**. Add a **Phone** and **Title** if you like, and tick **Make admin** for anyone who should help run the company.
4. Tap **Add person**.

Someone who already has an account is added straight away. Someone without one is added and waiting: you can put them on projects and give them tasks right away, and they join automatically when they sign up with that email.

## Send an invite

There's no email server yet, so the invite goes from your own email:

1. Select the person in the People table. Their form opens under the table.
2. Tap **Email invite** to open your email app with the message written, or **Copy invite** to paste it into a text or chat.

The link in the invite opens sign-up with their name and email filled in. What they see is explained in [Joining a production](/help/getting-started/joining-a-production).

## Change someone's details

Select a person to open their form. Company admins can change:

- **Name**, **nickname** and **phone**.
- **Title**, such as Line producer.
- **Admin**, which lets them manage the company.
- **Company roles**, picked from the role groups. See [Roles](/help/company/roles).
- **Other emails and phones**, for a work email or a second number.

Tap **Save** when you're done. Title and roles can also be changed in the table.

Only the person can change their sign-in email, from [Your profile](/help/company/your-profile).

## Remove someone

In their form, tap **Remove from company**. They come off all of the company's projects. Their tasks, notes and history stay. Someone still waiting to sign up is deleted when they leave their last company.

## Tips

- Give people their company roles before putting them on a project. Their roles are copied to the project when they're added.
- Phone numbers are stored as 919-555-0123. Numbers outside the US start with +.
- The **Account** column shows **Signed up** or **Not yet**, so you can see who still needs an invite.
