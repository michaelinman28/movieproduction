The Activity log records every add, change and delete across the company and its projects, plus sign-ins and each time a call sheet is opened. Only company admins can see it.

{{shots activity}}

## Where to find it

- **Whole company**: on the company page, tap **Activity**.
- **One project**: open **Activity** in the project's menu (admins only).
- **One item**: open a task, scene, shoot day, location or person and open its **History** section.

## Reading it

Each line says when, who, what they did and to which item, newest first, with what changed from what to what. Select a line to open that item, if it still exists.

The log comes a page at a time, with the pager under the table. Filters and search cover the whole log, not just the page you're on: search looks in item names, who did it and the details.

## What it records

- Every add, change and delete, however it was made, including deletes that take other items with them.
- Sign-ins.
- Each opening of a call sheet: in the app, through a personal link, or through the shared link.
- Turning a shared link on or off.

Private journal entries show only that one was added, changed or deleted, never what they say.

## How long it keeps things

Forever. A deleted project's history stays in the log. Nobody can change or delete entries one by one.

## Clean up the log

While the platform is being set up, admins can remove old entries with **Clean up the log** at the bottom of the page. Pick the project, kind of item and dates, then tap **Remove entries…** and confirm. Each clean-up leaves its own entry saying who removed how many. This tool will be switched off once the log is in real use.
