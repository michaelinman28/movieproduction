Roles say what people do: Director, Gaffer, Script Supervisor and so on. They group the crew by department on call sheets, and they decide who counts as cast and who as crew.

{{shots roles}}

## Where to find it

On the company page, tap **Manage roles** (or **Roles** if you're not an admin).

## Standard roles

Every company gets the same 32 standard film roles, grouped by department: Producing, Directing, Writing, Camera, Lighting, Grip, Art, Wardrobe, Sound, Casting, Locations, Post, Cast and Accounts. They can't be changed or deleted, so every company speaks the same language.

## Add a custom role

Company admins add roles their productions need that the standard list doesn't have, such as Drone Operator or Intimacy Coordinator.

1. Under **Custom roles**, type the role's name, and a description if you like.
2. Pick its department, or choose **New department…** to start one.
3. Tap **Add role**.

A custom role can't reuse a standard role's name.

## Change or delete a custom role

- Double-tap (or double-click) a cell to rename the role, or to change its department or description.
- Select a role to open its form, where you can also change it and tap **Save**, or tap **Delete role** to remove it. People who had it keep their other roles.

## Company roles and project roles

- **Company roles** are what someone usually does. Admins set them on the People list.
- **Project roles** are what they do on one project, set on that project's **Team** page.

When someone is added to a project, their company roles are copied to it as a starting point. Change the project roles afterwards without touching the company ones.

## Cast or crew

Roles also decide how someone sees call sheets:

- **Cast**: plays a character, or has a role in the Cast department. Sees their own call.
- **Crew**: any other role, and company admins. Sees the full sheet.
- **Both**: gets a switch between My call and Full sheet.

More in [Call sheets](/help/project/call-sheets).
