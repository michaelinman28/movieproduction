Your profile holds the details everyone else sees: on team lists, call sheets and task assignments.

{{shots profile}}

## Where to find it

Tap your name, next to the person icon, at the top of any page.

## Change your details

1. Change your **Full name**, **Nickname** or **Phone number**.
2. Tap **Save profile**.

Your nickname, if you have one, is the name people see first in the app.

## Change your sign-in email

1. Under **Sign-in email**, type the **New email**.
2. Tap **Send confirmation**.
3. Open the email sent to the new address and tap its link. Until then, you sign in with the old one.

Only you can change your sign-in email.

## What admins can change

A company admin can correct your name, nickname and phone, and add other emails and phones for you, from the company's People list. See [People and invites](/help/company/people).

## Dietary needs

Dietary needs are set per project, on the project's **Team** page, so catering sees them on that project's call sheets. See [Team](/help/project/team).
