A company holds your people, your roles and lists, and all your projects. You can belong to several companies, and each one is separate.

{{shots companies}}

## Where to find it

**Your production companies** is the first page after you sign in. Tap **Open** on a company's row to go to its page. From any project, the company's name at the top and the links under the project menu also lead there.

## Create a company

1. On **Your production companies**, type the name in **New company name**.
2. Tap **Create company**.
3. The company opens with its details form. Add a description and tap **Save**.

You become the company's first admin.

## Change the company's details

- **In the table**: on Your production companies, an admin double-taps (or double-clicks) the name or description to type a change.
- **In the form**: on the company page, an admin taps **Edit company**, changes the name or description, and taps **Save**.

## The company page

The company page has two sections:

- **Projects**: every project the company runs. Admins see them all; everyone else sees the projects they're on. See [Projects and the Overview](/help/project/projects-and-overview).
- **People**: everyone in the company. See [People and invites](/help/company/people).

Buttons at the top lead to **Roles**, **Task categories and tags**, and, for admins, **Activity**.

## Leave or delete a company

- **Leave company**, in your own line's form on the People list, takes you out of it. Your past work stays in its projects.
- **Delete company** (admins, in the Edit company form) removes the company and everything in it: projects, tasks, scenes and call sheets. The Activity log keeps a record of what was deleted.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Create a company | Anyone signed in |
| Change its name and description | Company admins |
| See its people | Everyone in the company |
| Delete it | Company admins |
