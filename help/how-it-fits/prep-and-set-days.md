Different people live on different pages. This page shows where each role usually starts, so you know where to look.

## In prep

| Role | Opens first | What they do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Producer | Overview, Tasks | Watch overdue tasks and the next shoot day; assign work |
| Assistant director | Scenes, Shoot days | Order scenes into days; build the schedule and the calls |
| Location manager | Locations, Tasks | Add and confirm locations; contacts, parking, hospitals; permits as tasks |
| Department heads | Elements, Tasks | Source props, wardrobe and effects; move each element from Needed to Ready |
| Director and DP | Scenes | Write shot lists for each scene |
| Company admin | Company page, Team | Add people, give roles, put people on projects |

**Tip:** on Tasks, choose **Show › Grouped by person** to see everyone's load, or **My tasks** for your own.

## The day before

1. The AD checks the day's **Schedule** and **Who's called**, and taps **Look up weather, sunrise and sunset** again for a fresh forecast.
2. **Open call sheet**, check it, and **Send version N**.
3. Send **Personal links**, or turn on the day's **Shared link** for people without one.
4. Watch confirmations come in on the call sheet page, and nudge anyone still Waiting.

## On set

- **Cast and crew** open their link or the call sheet page on a phone. Addresses open the phone's maps app.
- **The script supervisor or AD** ticks shots **Done** in each scene's shot list, and sets the scene to **Shot** when it's finished.
- **Anyone** can add a note to a scene or task. It's listed on **Project notes** for everyone to see.

## After wrap

- The **Overview** shows scenes, pages and shots done so far.
- Unfinished scenes go onto a later day from **Shoot days**.
- Company admins can see every change made during the day in the **Activity log**.
