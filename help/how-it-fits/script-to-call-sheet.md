summary: One walk-through using the sample film, from breakdown to a confirmed call.

---
This walk-through follows the sample project, **Movie #1**: Ellie gets a drone for her birthday, loses it over a lake, and finds it with her dad, Mark, on a trail. The steps are the same for any film.

## 1. Break the script into scenes

On **Scenes**, add each scene in script order with **Add scene**. For each one, fill in the set, INT or EXT, day or night, and the length in eighths of a page.

> Scene 3, EXT. BOND PARK - BOATHOUSE, day, 2 2/8 pages: "Ellie flies out over the lake. A gust takes the drone toward the trees."

Drag scenes to reorder them. Every scene starts as **Not scheduled**.

## 2. Add the characters

On **Characters**, add Ellie, Mark, Grace and Ranger Dale. Give each a cast number (Ellie is 1), and choose who plays them from the project's team. Then tick each scene's characters in the scene's form.

Cast numbers set the order the cast is listed on every call sheet.

## 3. List the elements

On **Elements**, add what each scene needs: the hero drone, rowboats, a safety harness for the tree climb, background passengers at the station. Give each a category, an owner and a status (Needed, Sourced, Ready), and link it to its scenes.

A prop that appears in four scenes is one element linked four times, so its status is always the same everywhere.

## 4. Lock the locations

On **Locations**, add the home, the Bond Park boathouse, the Umstead trails and Raleigh Union Station. Each has an address, on-site contact, parking and nearest hospital. Mark them **Confirmed** once they're locked, then set each scene's location.

## 5. Plan the prep

On **Tasks**, add what has to happen first: "Get a filming permit for Umstead", with sub-tasks for the application and the fee, due before the shoot and assigned to the location manager. The **Gantt chart** shows how the prep lines up against the shoot days.

## 6. Build the shoot day

On **Shoot days**, add December 7. In its form:

1. **Details**: crew call, shooting call, lunch and wrap. Add the home as the location, then **Look up weather, sunrise and sunset**, and **Save day**.
2. **Schedule**: add scenes 1, 2 and 7, with lunch between them. Each scene becomes **Scheduled**.
3. **Who's called**: add the cast and crew. A person with a character is listed as cast. Give each a call time, and pickup or makeup times for the cast.

## 7. Send it and watch it come back

1. **Open call sheet** shows the finished sheet: times, locations, hospital, weather, scenes with their cast and elements, cast and crew lists, and an advance look at the next day.
2. Tap **Send version 1**.
3. Send people their **Personal links** by email or text, or tap **Email everyone**.
4. As people open their links, their status moves from Waiting to Viewed to Confirmed.

If the plan changes, edit the day and send version 2. Everyone is asked to confirm again, and anyone still on version 1 shows as "Confirmed an old version".

## 8. On the day

On set, open the scene and tick each shot **Done** as it's shot. When a scene is finished, set it to **Shot**. The project's **Overview** counts scenes, pages and shots done.
