Everything you set up flows down into the call sheet. Each fact is entered once, in its own place, and every page that needs it reads it from there.

{{big-picture}}

## The chain, step by step

1. **Company.** Your people, their roles, your task categories and tags live here, shared by all your projects.
2. **Team.** Putting people on a project copies their company roles to it. Only people on the team can be given tasks, play characters or be called for a day.
3. **Plan the shoot.** Tasks, scenes, characters, elements and locations are the project's working lists. They point at each other: a scene lists its characters and elements and has a location; a character is played by someone on the team.
4. **Shoot days.** A day's schedule picks scenes from the Scenes page. Its locations come from the Locations page, and its calls come from the Team.
5. **Call sheet.** The sheet is built from the day and everything it points at. Nothing on it is typed twice.
6. **Links and print.** The sent sheet reaches people as personal links, one shared link or paper.

## What changes where

Because each fact lives in one place, a change made there shows up everywhere that uses it.

| Change this | On | It also updates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who plays a character | Characters, Team or a day's calls | Every call sheet the character is on |
| A location's address or contact | Locations | Tasks at that location, and every call sheet that uses it |
| A scene's characters or elements | Scenes or Elements | The cast and elements listed for that scene on call sheets |
| Someone's phone number | Company People or their profile | Every call sheet and team list |
| A scene put on a shoot day | Shoot days | The scene's status becomes Scheduled, and its location is added to the day |

## Links between pages

Wherever an item appears away from its own page, its name is a link. A scene on a call sheet opens the Scenes page with that scene's form open. A location on a task opens Locations. After following a link, **‹ Back to …** at the top takes you back to where you were, with your form open again.

## Alongside the chain

- **Notes.** Notes on tasks, scenes, shots, shoot days and calls stay with what they're about. **Project notes** lists them all, newest first, each with a link back.
- **History.** Every change is recorded. Company admins see it in the **Activity log**, and on the **History** section of each item's form.
