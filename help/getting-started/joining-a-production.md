---
title: Joining a production
summary: Accept an invite, create your account, and find your projects.
order: 5
---
A company admin adds you by your email address. You're part of the company and its projects as soon as you sign up with that same email.

## If you got an invite

1. Open the link in the invite. It opens **Create an account** with your name and email filled in.
2. Add your phone number if you like, and choose a password.
3. Tap **Create account**, then open the confirmation email and tap its link.
4. Sign in. Your company and projects are already there, along with any tasks you were given.

> Use the email address the invite was sent to. Signing up with a different one creates a separate account that your production can't see.

## If you already have an account

Sign in as usual. When an admin adds your email to their company, the company shows up on your companies list.

## If nobody has added you yet

You can create an account first with **New here? Create an account** on the sign-in page. You'll see no companies until an admin adds your email. Send them the email address you signed up with.

## Your profile

Tap your name (the person icon) at the top of the page to open **Your profile**. There you change your name, nickname and phone number. To change your sign-in email, type the new one and tap **Send confirmation**, then confirm it from your inbox.

Company admins can also correct your name and phone, and add other emails and phones for you. Only you can change your sign-in email.

## What you can see

- Every company you belong to, and the people in it.
- Every project you're on. Company admins see all of the company's projects.
- The Activity log is for company admins only.
