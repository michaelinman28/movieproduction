This is the shortest path from a new account to a sent call sheet. Each step takes a few minutes. You can stop after any step and come back later.

## 1. Create your company

1. Sign in, or create an account with your name, email and a password. Confirm your email from the message you receive.
2. On **Your production companies**, type your company's name and tap **Create company**.
3. The company opens with its details form. Add a description if you like, and tap **Save**.

You're the company's first admin. Admins add people, create projects and manage the company's lists.

## 2. Add your people

1. On your company's page, open **People**.
2. Type each person's **Email** and tap **Add person**.
3. If they haven't signed up yet, also give their **Name**. They're added now, and they join automatically when they sign up with that email.
4. Open a person to send them an invite: **Email invite** opens your own email app with the message written, and **Copy invite** copies it to paste anywhere.

> Give people their **Company roles** (Producer, Director, Gaffer…) now. When you put someone on a project, their company roles are copied to the project for you.

## 3. Create a project

1. On the company page, under **Projects**, type the project's name and tap **Create project**.
2. The project opens on its **Overview**. Fill in the logline, status and dates, and tap **Save**.
3. Open **Team** in the project's menu, choose each person, and tap **Assign to project**.

## 4. Break down the script

1. **Characters**: add each character, give them a cast number, and choose who plays them.
2. **Locations**: add each place you might shoot, with its address, on-site contact, parking and nearest hospital. Mark it Considering, Confirmed or Not using.
3. **Scenes**: add each scene with its INT/EXT, day or night, pages in eighths, characters and location. Add a shot list in the scene's form.
4. **Elements**: add props, wardrobe, vehicles and the rest, and link each one to the scenes that use it.

## 5. Plan tasks

On **Tasks**, add what needs doing before the shoot, with sub-tasks, due dates and the person responsible. Use the **Show** menu to see them as a calendar, a Gantt chart, an overdue list or grouped by person.

## 6. Schedule a shoot day and send the call sheet

1. On **Shoot days**, pick a date and tap **Add shoot day**.
2. In the day's form, fill in **Details** (times and locations), tap **Look up weather, sunrise and sunset**, and tap **Save day**.
3. Under **Schedule**, add the day's scenes in order, plus meal breaks and company moves.
4. Under **Who's called**, add people one by one, or tap **Call everyone**. Give each person a call time.
5. Tap **Open call sheet**, check it, and tap **Send version 1**.
6. Tell people it's ready: **Email everyone**, or send each person their own link from **Personal links**.

Each person's line shows Waiting, Viewed or Confirmed, so you can see who still needs a nudge.
