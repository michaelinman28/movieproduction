Once a company admin has added you to a project, everything for that project is one tap from your companies list.

## Get in

1. Sign in with the email your production added you with. If you don't have an account yet, see [Joining a production](/help/getting-started/joining-a-production).
2. Tap your company, then your project under **Projects**. You see the projects you're on.
3. The project opens on its **Overview**: open and overdue tasks, the next shoot day and how many scenes are shot.

The project's menu runs down the left on an iPad or computer, and across the top on a phone.

## Your tasks

1. Open **Tasks**.
2. In the **Show** menu, choose **My tasks** to see only what's assigned to you, soonest due first.
3. Tap a task to open its form under the table. Change its status, add a note, or tick its sub-tasks done.
4. Tap **Save** to keep changes made in the form. Changes typed straight into the table are saved as soon as you finish.

A task turns red once it's overdue. The **Overdue** view lists every late task on the project.

## Scenes, locations and elements

- **Scenes** lists the script in order. Tap a scene to see its characters, elements, location and shot list. On set, tick each shot **Done** as it's shot.
- **Locations** has each address. Tap **Open in maps** to get directions in your phone's maps app.
- **Elements** lists every prop, costume, vehicle and effect, who owns it and whether it's Needed, Sourced or Ready.

Anyone on the project can add to and change these pages.

## Your call sheet

When a day's call sheet is sent, you get it by email or text, or you find it under **Shoot days** with **Open call sheet**.

1. Your own call is at the top: your call time, and any pickup or makeup time.
2. Tap **Confirm I got it** so the production knows you've seen it.
3. If a new version is sent, you'll be asked to confirm again.

Crew see the full sheet. Cast see their own call, with **My call** and **Full sheet** to switch if they're both.

## Notes

Add notes to a task or a scene from its form. Every note on the project, wherever it was written, is listed on **Project notes**, with a link back to where it was taken.
