Movie Production is where a production company plans and runs its films. Everyone works from the same information: the team, the tasks, the script breakdown, the locations and the shooting schedule. Each shoot day's call sheet is built from all of it, so nothing is typed twice.

## What you can do

- **Run your company**: add your people, give them roles, and see every change in the Activity log.
- **Plan each project**: tasks and sub-tasks with due dates, a calendar and a Gantt chart.
- **Break down the script**: scenes with shot lists, characters and who plays them, and elements such as props and wardrobe.
- **Scout and lock locations**: addresses that open in your maps app, on-site contacts, parking and the nearest hospital.
- **Schedule the shoot**: shoot days with times, weather, locations, the order of scenes and who's called.
- **Send call sheets**: send each day's sheet, see who has viewed and confirmed it, and print it or save it as a PDF.

## Who uses it

| You are | You'll mostly use |
| --- | --- |
| A producer or company admin | Your company's people, roles and projects, and everything inside each project |
| On the crew | Your project's tasks, scenes, locations and shoot days |
| In the cast, or a day player | Your call sheet link. You don't need an account for it |

Start with the guide that fits you: [Quick start for producers](/help/getting-started/quick-start-producers), [Quick start for crew](/help/getting-started/quick-start-crew), or [Your call sheet link](/help/getting-started/your-call-sheet-link).

## Where it works

It runs in a web browser, on an iPad, a phone or a computer. There's nothing to install. Tables, forms and call sheets all fit a phone screen, and the switch at the top right turns on light or dark mode.

## How the parts connect

Everything you set up flows down into each day's call sheet. [The big picture](/help/how-it-fits/big-picture) shows how, and [From script to call sheet](/help/how-it-fits/script-to-call-sheet) walks through one production from start to finish.
