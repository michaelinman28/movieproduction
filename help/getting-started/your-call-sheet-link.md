Your production sent you a link to a call sheet. It opens in any web browser, on a phone or a computer, and you don't need an account or a password.

## What the call sheet tells you

A call sheet is the plan for one shoot day. From the top:

- **The day**: the project, which day of the shoot it is, the date and the crew call.
- **Your call**: the time you need to be there, and any pickup or makeup time. It's at the top of a personal link.
- **Times**: crew call, shooting call, lunch and the estimated wrap.
- **Locations**: each place you're shooting, with the address (tap it to open your maps app), parking and the on-site contact.
- **Nearest hospital** for each location, and the **weather**, sunrise and sunset.
- **Schedule**: the scenes in the order they'll be shot, with meal breaks and company moves.
- **Cast and crew**: who's called, and when. If you're only in the cast, a personal link shows just your own call.
- **Notes**, and an **advance schedule** of the next shoot day.

Times, locations, weather, the hospital and key contacts are grouped under **Day details**. Tap any heading, **Day details** or **Schedule** for example, to fold that part away; a short summary stays beside the heading. Your phone remembers what you folded.

## Confirm you got it

On a personal link, tap **Confirm I got it** near the top. The production then sees you've confirmed this version.

If they change the plan and send a new version, the same link shows the new sheet and asks you to confirm again.

## Two kinds of link

| Link | Who it's for | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal link** | Only you. Please don't forward it | The sheet with your own call at the top, and Confirm I got it |
| **Shared link** | Anyone the production shares it with | The whole day's sheet, view only, no confirming |

Neither link shows anyone's email address. Phone numbers show on a shared link only if the production chose to include them.

## Print it or save it as a PDF

Tap **Print or PDF** at the top. If the production prints call sheets two ways, there are two buttons: **Print modern** for the full design, and **Print 1 page** for the whole day on one page. The sheet is laid out on US Letter paper in light colors, with every part printed, even ones you folded. To keep a copy on your phone, choose **Save as PDF** (or **Save to Files** on an iPhone) in the print screen.

## If the link doesn't work

"This call sheet isn't available" means one of these:

- The sheet hasn't been sent yet.
- You've been taken off that day, so your personal link stopped working.
- The production turned off the shared link, or made a new one.

Ask the person who sent it for a new link.

## Want an account?

You don't need one to read your call sheet. If your production adds you to a project, an account lets you see your tasks and every shoot day in one place. See [Joining a production](/help/getting-started/joining-a-production).
