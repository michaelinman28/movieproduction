Wherever an item shows up away from its own page, its name is a link. Following it opens the item's page with that item selected and its form open.

{{shots links-back}}

## Examples

- A scene on a shoot day or call sheet opens **Scenes** with that scene's form.
- A location on a task opens **Locations**.
- A person on a call sheet opens **Team**.
- A task on the Overview opens **Tasks**.
- A note on Project notes opens the item it was written on.

In tables, dropdown cells show a small open arrow for the item picked. In forms, an open link sits under the picker.

## Back to where you were

After following a link, **‹ Back to …** at the top of the page names where you came from, such as "Back to Day 1". Tap it to go back, with the form you had open opened again. It's also in the pinned bar once you scroll.

## Help links

The **Help** link in the header opens these pages in a new tab, so nothing you're typing is lost.
