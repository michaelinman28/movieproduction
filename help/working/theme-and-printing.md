## Light and dark

Tap the moon or sun at the top right of any page to switch between light and dark. Until you tap it, the app follows your device's setting. Your choice is remembered on that device.

## Print a call sheet

On a call sheet, tap **Print or save as PDF** (or **Print or PDF** on a link). The sheet is laid out on US Letter paper, always in light colors, without the app's buttons and menus.

## Save as a PDF

In the print screen:

- **iPad and iPhone**: tap Share (or pinch out on the preview), then **Save to Files**.
- **Mac**: choose **Save as PDF** at the bottom left.
- **Windows and Chrome**: choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.

## Other pages

Other pages print what's on screen. For a list to share, a task summary is often better: tick tasks and tap **Email summary** or **Copy summary**. See [Tasks](/help/project/tasks).

## The footer

The footer on every page shows the app's version number. If something looks wrong, tell your admin the version you see.
