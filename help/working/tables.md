Every list in the app is a table that works the same way: tasks, scenes, people, locations and the rest.

{{shots tables}}

## Change a value in the table

- **Dropdown cells** (status, person, category, location) show a small arrow. Tap once to open the list, then pick. It saves at once.
- **Text, dates and numbers**: double-tap (or double-click) the cell, type, then tap elsewhere or press Enter. It saves as soon as you finish.
- **Lists of names** such as roles and tags are typed comma-separated, like "urgent, permits".
- **Lists picked from other items**, such as a scene's characters, open a dropdown of tick boxes.

Anything you can change in the table you can also change in the row's form. See [Forms](/help/working/forms).

## Select a row

Tap a row to select it. Its form opens under the table, and the page scrolls down to it.

## Filter and sort

- The row under the headers holds a filter for each column.
- Columns with fixed values, such as status, filter with a list of tick boxes and counts.
- Lists of names, such as roles and tags, let you tick each name.
- Text, dates and numbers filter by typing.
- Tap a column header to sort by it; tap again to reverse.

## Choose your columns

Tap **Columns** above a table to show or hide columns, or **Show all columns**. Drag a header to move a column, or its edge to resize it. Your view of each table is kept until you close the browser tab.

## Long lists

Most tables load everything and scroll. The two that keep growing, the Activity log and Project notes, come a page at a time with a pager under the table.

## On a phone

The table scrolls sideways as a whole, so a row's cells stay lined up. See [Phones and iPads](/help/working/phones-and-ipads).
