The app is built for an iPad first and works on a phone. Nothing needs installing: open it in Safari or Chrome.

{{shots phone}}

## On an iPad

- The project menu runs down the left.
- Rows, buttons and filters are sized for fingers.
- Tap where a computer would click, and double-tap where it would double-click.
- Dragging works for reordering scenes, shots and the schedule.

## On a phone

- The project menu runs across the top. Swipe it sideways to see every page.
- Tables scroll sideways as a whole. Swipe the table to see more columns.
- Forms stack their fields in one column.
- The calendar shows a count per day; tap a day to see its tasks.
- Call sheets are laid out to read on a phone.

## Add it to your home screen

In Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. The app then opens from an icon like any other app.

## Tips

- Fold away sections you don't use. Each device remembers what you folded.
- On set, the Scenes page's shot list is the quickest way to tick shots done.
- Addresses open your phone's maps app for directions.
