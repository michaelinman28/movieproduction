Selecting a row opens its form under the table, with every field of that item. A form holds your changes until you save.

{{shots forms}}

## Save or undo

- Make any changes, picks included, then tap **Save** (or **Save scene**, **Save day** and so on).
- **Undo changes** puts the form back the way it was.
- "Unsaved changes" shows while there's something to save.

Actions such as adding a note or a sub-task, or ticking a shot done, happen at once.

## The pinned Save button

While a form is open, its Save button stays pinned under the page header as you scroll. It reads **No changes** until you change something. If the form is out of sight, a link beside it jumps back to the form.

When no form is open, the page's main Add button pins there instead.

## You won't lose your work

If you try to select another row, add something new or leave the page with unsaved changes, the app asks first.

## After adding something

The new item is selected, its row is highlighted and its form opens, ready for the rest of its details.

## Sections

Each part of a page and each part of a form is a section with its own heading. Tap a heading to fold or unfold it. Your device remembers which ones you folded.
